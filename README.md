## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Privacy Badger** is a powerful Firefox extension designed to keep your online activities private while you browse the internet. Developed by the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF), this tool effectively blocks tracking cookies and advertisements that threaten your privacy. With the **latest version**, you can enjoy enhanced protection against unwanted tracking, ensuring a safer online experience. Download now to take full advantage of all features available!

## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Ad Blocking** — Blocks intrusive adverts that compromise your privacy while browsing.
- **Tracking Cookie Prevention** — Eliminates tracking cookies that monitor your online activities.
- **Color-Coded Security Levels** — Easily identify risks with a three-tier color code system:
  - **Green**: Safe adverts that don't register your data.
  - **Yellow**: Doubtful adverts that may send cookie information.
  - **Red**: Blocked adverts that violate your privacy.
- **User-Friendly Interface** — Intuitive design for easy navigation and effective management of privacy settings.
- **Regular Updates** — Ensures you always have the latest features and security improvements.
